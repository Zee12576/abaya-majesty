Shipping & Delivery Policy

MADE TO ORDER · CUSTOMISED WITH CARE · WORLDWIDE ENQUIRIES
Last updated: 14 November 2026

Made-to-order production timeline

Please allow up to 14 days for production after full payment has been received and the order details have been confirmed. Shipping transit time is additional and depends on destination and delivery service.

1. Where we deliver

We accept enquiries for delivery worldwide, subject to courier service availability, destination restrictions and applicable laws. Before payment, please confirm that delivery is available to your address.

2. Full payment and order confirmation

Full payment is required before production begins. Production starts only after payment is received and Abaya Majesty has confirmed the design, size or custom measurements, customisation details, delivery address and other order particulars. Please check your order confirmation carefully.

3. Production time

Please allow up to 14 days for production after full payment and confirmation of all order details. This is the production period only and does not include shipping transit time. If an unusual delay arises, we will make reasonable efforts to inform you.

4. Shipping time and charges

Shipping time varies according to destination, courier, customs processing and local delivery conditions. The shipping charge and estimated transit time will be communicated and confirmed before payment. Production and shipping are separate stages.

5. Tracking and delivery

Where the selected courier provides tracking, we will share tracking details after dispatch. Tracking updates are provided by the courier and may not appear immediately. Delivery is made to the address supplied and confirmed by the customer. Please ensure the recipient name, phone number, postal code and full address are accurate.

6. Customs duties, taxes and import requirements

International shipments may be subject to customs duties, import taxes, clearance fees or other charges imposed by the destination country. These vary by country. Customers should check the rules that apply at their destination before ordering. Any applicable destination-country charges will be communicated where known; charges imposed by local authorities or carriers may be payable by the recipient.

7. Delays and delivery issues

Delivery estimates are not guarantees. Delays may occur due to customs inspections, weather, public holidays, courier operations, address issues or other circumstances outside our reasonable control. If tracking shows an unusual delay or delivery problem, contact us and we will help check with the courier.

8. Incorrect address or failed delivery

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address and reachable contact number. If delivery fails or a parcel is returned because the supplied address or contact details were incorrect or the recipient was unavailable, additional shipping or re-delivery charges may apply. We will discuss available options with you.

Please note: The 14-day period refers to production, not total delivery time. The shipping estimate and shipping fee for your destination will be confirmed before you pay. Mandatory rights under applicable law remain unaffected.
