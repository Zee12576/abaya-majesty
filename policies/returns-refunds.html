Our made-to-order approach

Each order is prepared specifically after the design, size and order details are confirmed. We do not accept change-of-mind returns or exchanges. This policy does not remove rights that cannot legally be excluded under applicable consumer law.

1. No change-of-mind returns or exchanges

Because our abayas are made to order, we do not accept returns or exchanges simply because a customer changes their mind, no longer wants the item, or prefers a different colour, style or fit after confirming the order. Please review the design, size, measurements, customisation details and final order confirmation carefully before paying.

2. Customised and made-to-order items

Orders made to customer-provided measurements or with customer-requested customisation are prepared according to the details approved by the customer. We cannot accept a return or exchange because measurements or preferences supplied or approved by the customer do not produce the fit or appearance the customer expected. Please contact us before confirming your order if you need help with measurements.

3. Incorrect, damaged or defective items

If your order arrives damaged, has a manufacturing defect, or is materially different from the order details confirmed by Abaya Majesty, contact us as soon as possible and preferably within 48 hours of delivery. Provide your order details and clear photographs or video of the issue, and packaging photographs where relevant. We will review the information and explain the next steps. Where required by applicable law, we will provide an appropriate remedy, which may include repair, replacement or refund.

Required unboxing evidence for damage or defect reports

An unboxing video or clear photographs are required when reporting an item as damaged or defective. The unboxing video should, where possible, be continuous and show the unopened parcel, its condition and shipping label before opening, followed by the opening of the package and the item received. If a video is not available, provide clear photographs of the unopened parcel, outer packaging, inner packaging and the damaged or defective area of the item. Please keep the product and all packaging until we have reviewed the report. This evidence helps us assess the claim; it does not remove or limit any mandatory consumer rights or remedies under applicable law.

4. How to report an issue

Email abayamajesty0312@gmail.com or WhatsApp +971 50 554 0491. Include your name, order reference, delivery date and a clear description of the issue, together with the required unboxing video or photographs. Do not send an item back before receiving return instructions from us.

5. Refunds

Where a refund is approved or required by applicable law, we will confirm the amount, method and expected processing steps. The time taken for funds to appear may depend on the payment provider or financial institution. Mandatory rights and remedies under applicable consumer-protection law remain unaffected.

6. Order cancellation

Contact us immediately if you need to request cancellation. Because production is arranged for each confirmed order, cancellation may not be possible once production has started. Any cancellation or refund will be assessed in accordance with the order status and applicable law. We will communicate the applicable position before accepting the order.

Important: This policy does not override mandatory consumer rights in India or any other country where we sell. If local law provides a non-waivable right to cancel, return, repair, replace or obtain a refund, that law applies.
